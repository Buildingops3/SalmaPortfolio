The Story

From the Clinic Door to Building the Function.

2019
2019 Chapter 1

Learning HCPs Face to Face

Medical Sales Representative, Cardiometabolic, AstraZeneca

Pharmaceutical sciences graduate, sales rep by first job. When COVID-19 hit, I helped roll out early remote detailing and hybrid engagement. My first taste of digital doing the job of a visit.

Why it matters: I know how HCPs decide, first-hand.

2020 Chapter 2

The Rep Who Pushed Digital

Senior Medical Sales Representative, Obesity, Novo Nordisk

I became the omnichannel focal point in the field team. I drove CRM-led and hybrid engagement adoption, and fed what I saw in clinics back into segmentation and channel sequencing.

Why it matters: I know what field teams will actually adopt.

2023 Chapter 3

From the Field Into Marketing

Marketing Assignee, Obesity Franchise, Novo Nordisk. Oct 2023 to Dec 2024, alongside my field role

I joined the brand team while still in the field. I co-led execution of the annual tactical marketing plan, bridging field insights with digital and content. I contributed to integrated marketing and content strategies as the brand shifted toward omnichannel, partnered with agencies on compliant digital assets and the HCP education platform, and supported national professional education and digital events.

Why it matters: I learned marketing inside a global brand team, with global standards and full medical, regulatory and legal review.

2025 Chapter 4

Leading Omnichannel

Omnichannel & Digital Content Lead, Obesity Franchise, Novo Nordisk. Jan 2025 to Apr 2026

I led omnichannel and digital content strategy for a major obesity brand across HCP and patient journeys, including the Wegovy launch. I managed agencies remotely through medical, regulatory and legal approvals, and used data to optimize sequencing and personalization across social, CRM, digital platforms and events.

Why it matters: I've led omnichannel inside a global company, not just studied it.

2026 Chapter 5

Building It From Scratch

Omnichannel Engagement & Digital Content Lead, Global Marketing Affairs, Eva Pharma. Markets: KSA, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar and Libya

A two-person team, no function, no consent base. Now: channel strategy, CRM architecture, a PDPL-aligned consent framework, and campaigns running across KSA, the GCC and Libya. The same year, I completed my MBA with the University of Suffolk, United Kingdom.

Why it matters: I can build the whole system, not just run the campaigns inside it.
